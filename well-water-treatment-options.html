Well Water Treatment Options at a Glance

No single device fixes every well. Treatment should follow test results, and many homes end up with two or three components in sequence. This is a high-level map, not a prescription.

Sediment filtration

Catches sand and grit, and often protects downstream equipment.

Oxidizing or specialty filters

Commonly used for iron, manganese and sulfur. Media choice depends on your water chemistry.

Activated or catalytic carbon

Used for taste, odor and certain chemicals. The two media behave differently. See activated carbon vs catalytic carbon for how they differ.

Water softeners

Address hardness through ion exchange. Some can handle small amounts of iron, but check the manufacturer's stated limits.

UV disinfection

Used to treat bacteria and other microorganisms. It generally needs clear water upstream to work well.

Reverse osmosis

Usually installed at one tap for drinking water. It reduces a broad range of dissolved contaminants.

For an organized overview of these system types, see well water treatment systems on Water System Guide.
