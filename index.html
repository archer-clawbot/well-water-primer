Living With a Private Well: Where to Begin

When your water comes from a private well, nobody else is monitoring it for you. Public water systems are tested and regulated; private wells generally are not, so the owner decides when to test and what to do about the results.

The short version: learn what your well's water actually contains, fix the problems the results point to, and keep testing over time.

Why well water changes

Groundwater picks up whatever is in the rock and soil around it, and nearby land use, weather and the condition of the well itself can affect it. Water that was fine when you bought the house isn't guaranteed to stay that way.

Three questions to keep in mind

  1. What's actually in my water, according to a recent test?
  2. Which of those findings is a health concern and which is mostly a nuisance?
  3. Which treatment targets that specific problem?

These notes cover each step. For deeper reference material on equipment and treatment, see Water System Guide.
